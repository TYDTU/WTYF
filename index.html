About the show

A living funeral, minus the beige sadness.

Welcome To Your Funeral is part comedy, part love letter, part existential group project. Each guest reflects on their own life, then hears messages, stories, roasts, tributes, and tiny emotional grenades from the people who know and love them.

It is funny, strange, vulnerable, awkward, and much better than waiting until someone is dead to say the thing that mattered.

Your hosts

Two brilliant women, one microphone, zero interest in waiting.

The traditional show

Two episodes. Some behind-the-scenes magic. One very alive guest.

Our original format gives a featured guest space to tell their story — and then come back to hear the good stuff.

01 · Episode one

The Obituary

A one-on-one conversation with our featured guest. We get to know the person behind the life story: their memories, relationships, and wonderfully human details.

Between episodes

We make our magic

Then we get to work behind the scenes, gathering stories, messages, tributes, and love from the people who know our guest. This is where their living funeral takes shape.

02 · Episode two

The Funeral

Our guest returns for their own living funeral: a chance to hear the stories, feel the love, and get their flowers while they are here to enjoy them.

In person is our favorite. Virtual is welcome. We love being in the same room. Right now, our production budget is $0, so we work with what is possible — including getting together virtually.

A new addition to the show

The Para Portion

A place to discuss all things para. Our first spin-off? Parasocial Relationships.

We admire them.
They have no idea who we are.

You know that feeling of knowing someone through their work, interviews, or public life — even though you have never actually met? That one-sided connection is a parasocial relationship. Virginia and Beth are taking theirs to the microphone, with affection and a healthy awareness that we are making a lot of this up.

Only positive admiration here. The imaginary friendship is ours; we are not pretending it is mutual.

01 · First episode

The admiration

We talk about someone we admire, what we appreciate about them, and the parasocial relationship we have built from afar. All love, no takedowns.

02 · Second episode

The imaginary obituary

Beth and Virginia answer mostly lighthearted obituary questions for that person, based on the completely made-up world in our heads. A playful, fictional exercise — not their real answers or biography.

Your turn

How close did we get?

Listeners guess how close they think we came, and why. What feels plausible? Where did our imaginary friendship take a spectacular detour?

A formal invitation, with absolutely no pressure

Come right our wrongs.

At the end of the episode, we formally invite our admired subject to join the traditional show: a real Obituary conversation, our behind-the-scenes magic, and their very own living Funeral.

Tell us what we got right. Set us straight on everything else. And if the invitation never reaches you, or you would rather pass? No hurt feelings. You have no clue who we are. LOL.

With admiration (and an entirely imaginary shared history),
Virginia & Beth
